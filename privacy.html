Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

PDF Library – Reader is designed to help you manage and read PDF documents. This policy describes the app's intended data practices. Please confirm these statements match the released app before publishing.

Information collection

The app does not require an account and is designed not to collect personal information, analytics, or advertising identifiers.

Documents and local storage

Imported PDFs, folders, bookmarks, highlights, and reading preferences are intended to be stored on your device. The app does not intentionally upload your documents to developer-operated servers.

Processing

PDF viewing and supported reading features are designed to run on-device. No document content is intentionally sent to external AI services.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell personal information or use third-party advertising trackers.

Deletion

You may remove documents using available app controls. Uninstalling the app generally removes app-local data, subject to device backups and files stored elsewhere.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences and is not designed to collect information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as features or practices change. Updates will be published here.
