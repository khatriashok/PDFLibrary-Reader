# Privacy Policy – PDF Library – Reader

**Effective Date: November 3, 2026**

PDF Library – Reader respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information and documents are handled when you use our application.

## 1. Information We Collect

PDF Library – Reader does not collect, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.

## 2. PDF Documents and Storage

PDF documents imported into the app are stored locally on your device.

Your documents, folders, bookmarks, highlights, and reading preferences remain under your control.

The app does not upload your PDF documents to developer-operated servers.

## 3. Document Processing

PDF viewing, text searching, highlighting, and other supported reading features are processed on your device.

The app does not transmit document contents to external AI services or third-party processing servers.

## 4. Analytics and Tracking

PDF Library – Reader does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or document contents with advertisers or data brokers.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Documents and related reading information are stored on your device. You can remove imported documents through the app's available file-management features.

Deleting the app generally removes its locally stored data, subject to operating-system backups and any files saved outside the app.

## 7. Children's Privacy

PDF Library – Reader is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Security

The app relies on the operating system's security protections to help safeguard locally stored documents.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or data-handling practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
