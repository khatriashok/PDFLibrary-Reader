Support Center

Need help with PDF Library – Reader? Here are answers to common questions.

How do I import a PDF?

Use the app's Import or Add control, then select a PDF from Files.

How do I organize files?

Use the library's folder controls, where supported by your installed version.

How do I zoom?

Pinch with two fingers while viewing a PDF to zoom in or out.

How do I highlight or bookmark?

Use the available reading tools in your app version. Some PDFs may not support text selection.

How can I fix a file that won't open?

Confirm the file is a valid PDF, check available device storage, restart the app, and try again. Password-protected files may require additional steps.

Contact support

Email a_khatri@yahoo.com with your device model, operating system version, and a description of the issue. Avoid sending sensitive PDFs unless necessary.
